Common questions

How do I log a match? Tap the + button on your Passport, choose the match, pick "I was there" or "I watched", and press the stamp.

Can I add the score later? Yes — stamp at kickoff and open the stamp anytime to add or edit the result.

Where is my data? Entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app removes your passport, so back up your phone to keep it.

A knockout fixture shows no teams. Bracket slots fill in once those matches are decided — until then, type the two teams yourself when you log it.

Matchday Passport is an independent fan app, not affiliated with FIFA or any tournament organizer.
