Privacy Policy — Matchday Passport

Last updated: 14 June 2026

Matchday Passport does not collect any data. The app works entirely on your device, with no account, no sign-up, and no network connection.

What we collect

Nothing. Matchday Passport has no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and makes no network requests. We never see, receive, or store any information about you or your activity.

What stays on your device

The matches you log — teams, scores, venues, dates, notes, and any photos you attach — are stored only in the app's private storage on your iPhone. This data never leaves your device. If you delete the app, this data is removed with it.

Photos

If you attach a photo to a match, the app uses the system photo picker. A copy of the photo you choose is saved inside the app's private storage on your device. The app does not request access to your full photo library and does not upload photos anywhere.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here.
